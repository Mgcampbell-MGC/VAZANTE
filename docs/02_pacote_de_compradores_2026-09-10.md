## Part 2 — The calibration pack

### 2.0 Cover note for GC — how to run the 45 minutes

The pack is not a form. Email it as a form and it comes back as a form, and a form tells you the buyer's marketing position. A session tells you where his price actually breaks. The whole value is in the sequence and in what you do when he hesitates.

Who has to be in the room: someone who can say a number out loud. Not IR, not BD, not "the person who covers Sutphin". If that person is not available, move the meeting; a V0 built with the wrong attendee has to be unwound later, and unwinding costs more than waiting. What you bring: one screen, thirty archetypes one at a time, a printed copy for them to scribble on. No deck, no NDA, no data. Everything is synthetic — say so in the first minute; it removes the only procedural excuse to postpone.

| Minutes | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | What VAZANTE is: a principal that buys whole broken books and resells the pieces. Not a broker. No mandate, no fee is being asked for. |
| 3–5 | The rule of the session: thirty standardised profiles; four answers each, ten seconds each. We are not asking you to define your mandate. |
| 5–10 | A01 and A02, the two anchors — everything clean, everything broken. Fixes the scale and takes the fear out of the exercise. A01 is also the S1 test (see 2.7). |
| 10–30 | The one-dimension ladders, in order: title → cash → DPD → debtor → recourse → existence. Twenty archetypes, a minute each. |
| 30–36 | The three carteiras. Portfolio construction, five questions each. |
| 36–42 | The evidence ladder — what makes you pay MORE. The highest-value six minutes in the session. |
| 42–45 | Execution: time to indicative, time to firm, approval, capacity, settlement, retrade. |

Never ask "do you buy X". Ask "would you buy this, and at what price — 4¢? 7¢? 10¢? What fact moves you from 4 to 10?"

"It depends" names the dimension you are about to isolate. Say "it depends on what?", write the answer verbatim, and show the next archetype, which varies exactly that thing. That is why the ladders are ordered.

No number → take a bracket. AUTO-BUY / RANGE / MANUAL REVIEW / PASS are all real answers; "I'd have to look" is not. The reply is "if you had to quote it today with nothing more, what would you not go above?"

Do not defend the archetypes. If he says one is unrealistic, write down why and move on. His objection is data about his book.

What you are harvesting, and only this: (a) the hard filters — where price stops mattering; (b) the price deltas per dimension — how many centavos each evidence state is worth to this buyer; (c) capacity and speed, without which no price can ever become locked proceeds. What you must not do: show him the Oracle's number for anything; tell him what another buyer said; print the evidence ladder into any deal tape later. Immediately after, the 20-minute write-up: fill the archetype table with his answers, tag each rule CONFIRMED (he said it) or INFERRED (you deduced it), and send Part 3 pre-filled with his numbers where he gave them and the house standard where he did not — within two business days, which is the difference between a signed V0 and a stalled thread.

## Pacote de Calibração de Comprador

VAZANTE — Sutphin Ltd. · documento de trabalho · todos os perfis abaixo são sintéticos · não requer NDA

O objetivo deste exercício não é pedir que o senhor defina o seu mandato do zero.

A VAZANTE compra carteiras de crédito inteiras, à vista, como principal, e revende as partes. Para fazer isso com responsabilidade, precisamos saber — antes de comprar — o que o senhor compra e quanto paga. Não estamos pedindo mandato, exclusividade nem honorário.

Vamos mostrar trinta perfis padronizados de ativo. Para cada um, quatro respostas apenas:

1. Decisão — COMPRA / ANÁLISE / PASSA
2. Preço indicativo — por R$1,00 de face corrente, ou uma faixa
3. Tamanho máximo — face ou exposição de preço de compra que o senhor tomaria com essas características
4. O que muda a resposta — o fato, um ou dois, que mais aumentaria ou reduziria o seu preço

Se uma restrição for flexível ao preço certo, marque DEPENDE DO PREÇO em vez de PASSA. Se o senhor genuinamente não sabe, marque ANÁLISE — é uma resposta legítima e útil.

A linguagem. Todos os perfis são descritos em cinco dimensões independentes, que a VAZANTE nunca combina em uma nota única:

| Dimensão | Estados |
|---|---|
| **E** — Existência — o crédito existe? | confirmada (NF-e autorizada na SEFAZ + hash digVal conferido) · parcial (chave íntegra, XML não entregue) · fraca (apenas planilha do cedente) |
| **T** — Título — a VAZANTE terá o direito que está vendendo? | confirmada (cessão registrada em registradora autorizada, sem duplicidade) · legado não registrado (cessão assinada e datada, sem registro — papel anterior ao regime pleno) · conflito (duplicidade registrada ou cessão sem assinatura) |
| **C** — Caixa — quem vem pagando de verdade? | % dos recebimentos dos últimos 24 meses originados do próprio sacado, por raiz-8 do CNPJ pagador no retorno CNAB. O restante é caixa do cedente, recompra ou não identificado |
| **D** — Devedor — realidade comercial do sacado | ativo · com protesto · em recuperação judicial · com vínculo societário, de endereço ou de contabilidade com o cedente |
| **R** — Regresso — existe alguém atrás do crédito? | nenhum · cláusula de regresso identificada no termo de cessão · aval dos sócios com firma reconhecida / confissão de dívida |

Ausência de registro não é sinal de inexistência. A maior parte das duplicatas anteriores ao regime escritural pleno não está registrada em registradora. A VAZANTE trata E e T como dimensões completamente separadas e pede que o senhor faça o mesmo ao responder.

### 2.7 The one reconciliation to make before the first session

The Premium house grid in Part 3.1 as first drafted (13–22¢) priced the aged C1 strip — the "C1 rest" bucket that the economics carry at 25¢. It did not price the S1 strip — a vencer or under 30 dpd, sacado-confirmed cash, title clean — which the economics carry at 75¢ and which is 43% of locked proceeds on the reference book on 5% of face. The grid now has both tiers. The A01 price is the S1 test. If Premium buyers put A01 at 20–25¢ rather than 70–80¢, the strip that carries the trade is worth a third of what the model assumes, the reference bid falls through the seller's floor, and the size floor moves to ≈R$95m documented. That is the business case's "number most likely to be wrong #1", and the calibration session is the cheapest place on earth to find it out. Do not leave the first Premium session without a number on A01.

## Part 3 — The three standing buy boxes

Each template is in two parts. Parte A is the box — pre-filled with VAZANTE's house standard, for the buyer to tick, strike or overwrite. Parte B is one page, and signing it converts the ticked Parte A into a standing monthly mandate: a binding commitment to buy, at the stated grid, up to the stated monthly capacity, anything VAZANTE delivers that meets the box, for the stated validity, refreshed monthly. Parte A is easy to sign because it looks like a description. Parte B is the commitment, it is short, and it is where the buyer's lawyer spends all his time. Do not merge them, and do not send Parte B before the session. House-standard numbers are HOUSE — consistent with the reference-book economics (S1 a vencer ~75¢; aged C1 ~13–25¢; recourse ~6–12¢; tail ~1–5¢; F/NV negative) — and open to MGC's override before anything is sent.

### 3.4 Notes on the backstop box — read before sending

**Why the carve-out right is written the way it is.** A standing "I'll buy everything that's left" is, from the buyer's side, an open invitation to adverse selection, and every competent counterparty sees it in the first thirty seconds. If the document does not answer it on its face, the answer becomes a negotiation and the commitment becomes indicative. So the box answers it three ways at once — a hard cap at 35% of documented face (now with the documented-face definition the first draft lacked), full disclosure of every carved line inside the same tape, and a freeze on carving after a quote is in. The freeze is the one that actually buys trust.

**Why F/NV is compulsory and priced at zero.** On the reference book F/NV face is negative at −2.0¢ — roughly 400 positions × R$688 of unavoidable handling (MODEL). If the backstop may leave it behind, VAZANTE ends every trade owning a handling liability with no exit, which converts a fast trade into exactly the recovery business absolute rule 5 forbids. Compulsory-at-zero, capped at 35% of the block, makes that liability someone else's, and the low grid price is the consideration. Say that out loud in the meeting. A backstop buyer who understands he is being paid in price to take the garbage will accept it; one who discovers it in the drafting will not.

**Why capacity accumulates here and nowhere else.** Deal flow is lumpy. A residual capacity that resets monthly is useless in the month a R$80m book lands; two months of carry-forward converts a R$3m/month commitment into a R$9m usable ceiling without asking the buyer to raise his annual limit. Cheapest concession to ask for, worth more than 0.5¢ on the grid.

**What to concede if it stalls, in order:** the 90-day grid validity (to 60); the exclusivity in Parte B §8 (narrow to books above R$20m face); the F/NV cap (35% → 25%, and 0.3¢ off the grid to compensate). **What never to concede:** compulsory F/NV inclusion; the closed retrade list; the freeze on carving after a quote; the arras. Those are what make a residual grid usable as locked proceeds. A grid that can be retraded for any reason is not locked proceeds; it is a hope, and it stays out of the numerator.

**Rule 4 check.** On the reference book the residual is roughly half of locked proceeds. That is already close to a breach of "no counterparty above 40–45%". Sign two residual grids before Deal 1 and split the block, or price the retrade option in — do not pretend it is not there.

## Part 4 — The machine-readable box

Every rule carries a confidence (CONFIRMED — the buyer said it · OBSERVED — derived from actual transactions · INFERRED — the compiler deduced it from examples or emails · UNKNOWN) and every price carries a status (FIRM · RECENT_OBSERVED · GRID · HISTORICAL · ESTIMATE). Only FIRM contributes to locked proceeds. That single line in the schema is what stops a grid from being mistaken for a contract. Two boxes exist per buyer at all times, stated and observed; the observed box dominates from deal 3 and the buyer never sees it. ## Part 6 — Deal 0 as the closing step of calibration

Deal 0 is not a rehearsal. It tests the one hypothesis the whole business rests on: that VAZANTE understands broken portfolios and buyers well enough to know the exit before it sets the entry price. Parts 1–5 produce stated boxes; Deal 0 is the first and only pre-money check on whether stated boxes predict real numbers. Its tapes are also the shadow portfolio of Stage 3 and the anonymised pool of 5.4 — one artefact, three uses. The book is Fictor Invest FIDC (public file, dated, with an observed outcome), supplemented by the BRB / Tirreno public record as an existence benchmark and, if Prisma will give it, one real anonymised tape under NDA. Run it as the paired question — parcel bids and whole-book bids in the same email — so the same exercise tests the manufacturing margin (business case condition 4).

### 6.1 The independence protocol

| Rule | The mechanic |
|---|---|
| No Oracle number, ever | No price, no band, no "we think it's around". If a buyer asks what VAZANTE thinks it is worth: "that's exactly what I'm not going to tell you, because then your number tells me nothing" — which is also a good answer commercially |
| Simultaneous release | All tapes leave the same mailbox within the same hour, same competency date, same anonymisation seed |
| No cross-reference | Nobody is told who else is looking. No "another buyer said". No count of participants |
| Sealed return | Written responses to a single mailbox by a stated deadline; nothing opened before it; nothing discussed with buyer 2 after buyer 1 has replied |
| Five business days | One extension of two days, available to all or to none |
| Same person asks all | GC. Same script, same order. A different asker is a different experiment |
| The residual is priced by all | Each buyer gets his own strip plus the entire residual block — three independent prices on one identical block, which isolates buyer variance from routing variance |
| Paired question | Each buyer is also asked what he would pay for the whole book, in the same email |

### 6.2 What each buyer receives and what gets compared

Premium receives the Premium strip (anonymised, position level, E/T/C/D/R per line, pull_id per field) plus the full residual block; Claims receives the Claims strip (recourse mapping, cedente qualification, aval / confissão inventory, RJ status by docket date) plus the full residual; the backstop receives the residual block with the carved lines flagged and visible. Each is asked buy / pass per line or sub-block, price per R$1.00, maximum size, what must be true before firm — and the whole-book price. Four comparisons, in the order of what they teach: total exit (Σ buyer exits, each strip from its natural buyer, residual from the best of three) versus the Oracle's predicted exit; strip by strip — a total that matches while the strips do not is a coincidence and will not repeat; residual variance across three buyers on an identical block — within ~15% means the residual grid is a real market price and the backstop can be trusted, 3× apart means there is no residual market and the 1.20× rule has no floor, the single most informative number the experiment produces, at zero extra cost; routing error rate — which positions each buyer excluded that the Oracle routed to him, and why; every reason is a hard filter the session failed to capture, written into the YAML with confidence OBSERVED.

### 6.3 The pass / fail rule

| Gap: Oracle exit vs buyer exits | Verdict | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Within ±10% | PROCEED | Stop analysing the business; go hunt the first transaction |
| 10–30% | RECALIBRATE | The gap is attributable — comparisons 2 and 4 name the dimension; fix the boxes; re-run on one strip only, on a different book. Never the same book: buyers have seen it and their second answer is contaminated |
| Above 30% | STOP AND LEARN | "If Oracle says R$11m and buyers say R$5m, stop engineering and learn why." No seller approach until the diagnosis is written and a second Deal 0 has passed |
| Paired: Σ parcel bids < 1.55× best whole-book bid on 2 of 3 books | NO GO | There is no manufacturing margin after tax (business case condition 4) |
| Fewer than 2 of 3 buyers put a number on paper | FAILED, whatever the one number says | One number is not a market, and the residual-variance comparison cannot be computed |

### 6.4 The honest problem with Deal 0, and the three corrections

Free indications on a deal that is not real are systematically too high: a buyer with no capital at risk, no committee paper filed and no expiry to defend quotes generously, because it costs him nothing and keeps him in the flow. Take those numbers at face value and the 1.20× rule is satisfied on paper and violated in fact. Three corrections, all applied: ask for the indicative on letterhead, addressed to Sutphin, with a stated expiry and a named signatory — a document with a name and a date goes past someone and removes about half the generosity; haircut un-signed numbers by 15% (HOUSE, ASSUMPTION) when computing the gap, stated in advance so the test cannot be argued about afterwards, and replace the 15% with the observed indicative-to-settled spread after deal 3; require at least one buyer — the backstop — to state a monthly capacity alongside the price, because a price without a capacity is an opinion, not an exit.

# Once a fund says yes

**14 September 2026. From the first yes to money in, in forty days.**

---

## First — what actually counts as a yes

*"Manda a página"* is not a yes. *"Vamos conversar"* is not a yes.

**Yes is: he agrees to give you the tape.** Everything before that is still selling. Do not start the
clock, and do not start work, until he has said it.

---

## The shape of it

**Two tracks running at the same time. Never one after the other.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Track A — the seller** | paper, then tape, then lots |
| **Track B — the buyers** | teaser, then NDA, then the round |

**Track B starts on day one, from public data. Do not wait for the tape.** This is where the weeks are.
Every broker runs these in sequence and takes three months. The lots we can describe without a tape —
the distressed block especially — can be in front of named buyers while the seller's lawyers are still
reading the corretagem.

---

## Track A — the seller

**Day 0–1 · NDA, both directions.**
Before the tape, never after. The moment a tape lands we are holding other people's personal data and
LGPD applies to us.

**Day 1–3 · The short-form corretagem.**
One page. **With the gestora as a company, never with the fund** — that keeps the whole thing outside
Anexo II da Res. CVM 175, so no regulamento change, no assembleia, no CVM filing. One director signs.
Mobilisation fee, **fully creditable against the success fee — if the book sells it costs him nothing.**
Say that in the first sentence, not the last.

**Day 1–3 · The tape spec goes out with the contract.**
One page of fields. Sending it afterwards costs a week for no reason.

**Day 3–8 · Read the tape.**
Reconcile it against his own CVM informe **first**. If the tape does not tie to what he filed, that is
finding number one and it goes back to him the same day. Then the offline sweep — CNPJ check digits,
NF-e chaves, duplicates, a sacado that is its own cedente, zero-face rows. Then cut the lots.

**Day 8–12 · Build the sale book.**
Per lot: what it is, the ageing, the concentration, every cedente resolved through the Receita, and an
inventory of the assignment documents. **Descriptive only. No opinion of value, anywhere, ever.**

---

## Track B — the buyers

**Day 1 · Teaser out.**
No name, no CNPJ. Shape only: *"a R$41m distressed block across four funds under one manager, 66%
recourse, nine named cedentes."* Every number true, nothing identifying. That paragraph is what buys
the NDA.

**Day 1–5 · NDAs back.**
Named buyers from the registry, matched to the lot. **Nobody gets exclusivity. Not for a day.**

**Day 12 · Data room opens** to whoever signed.

**Day 12–22 · The round.**
**One standard proposal form for everybody**: lot, price, conditions precedent, expiry, arras,
signatory. If each buyer bids on his own paper you cannot compare them, and comparison is the product.

**Day 22 · Round closes.** On a stated day, in writing, said on day one.

---

## The close

**Day 23 · The quadro comparativo.**
Every indication side by side, normalised, with what each buyer said would move his number. **This is
the thing he is actually paying for.**

**Day 23–25 · He picks.**

**Day 25–40 · Settlement.**
His counsel drafts the instrumento particular de cessão — we draft nothing. **The buyer pays the fund
directly. We never touch the money.** We invoice separately.

## The five things that kill it

**1. "Traga uma proposta primeiro e depois conversamos."**
The most likely single sentence you will hear, and it is worse than a no because it feels like progress.
**The answer is a fee small enough not to be a decision, and creditable.** Have the short-form in your
pocket before the call, not after it.

**2. The tape arrives broken or half-empty.**
The spec goes out **with** the contract. And tell him plainly: a missing sacado CNPJ is not a detail, it
is the difference between a lot that can be sold and one that cannot.

**3. The administrador blocks it.**
Pick houses where the gestor and the administrador are the same people. Libertas and Actual are the only
ones in the whole list, which is exactly why they are call number one.

**4. A buyer asks for exclusivity.**
No. The round is the product. A buyer who will not bid against others was never going to pay a real
number.

**5. Title.**
We warrant nothing about title and we say so on the first call. The buyer runs his own registradora
check — we have no contracts there and pretending otherwise would end the relationship.

---

## The rules that never bend

- **No price** out of a call, an email or a session. A price comes out of a round.
- **Never** *laudo*, *parecer*, *auditoria*, *avaliação*, *mandato*, or an opinion of value.
- **The gestora signs, never the fund.**
- **We never hold the money.**
- **We never buy.**
